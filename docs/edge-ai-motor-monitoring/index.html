Edge AI motor condition monitoring, end to end

Current, voltage, vibration and temperature from a DC motor are turned into 14 features on the microcontroller every 15 ms. A model then classifies each 450 ms window into 7 motor states and faults. I benchmarked 22 models on accuracy, false alarms, memory and speed and deployed 20 of them on the STM32. The winner, a multi-task MLP, reaches 97.1% on unseen recordings in 1.58 ms.
